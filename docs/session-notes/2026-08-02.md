## Product decisions made today

### Permanent About section

Mentavio needs a permanent About section for people who do not yet understand the product.

The section must explain:

- what Mentavio is;
- what the user does;
- who the platform is for;
- the educational purpose;
- the role of Sophia;
- that Mentavio is not a broker or real-money trading platform.

This requirement is preserved in:

- `docs/specs/SPEC-028-ABOUT-MENTAVIO.md`

### Financial glossary

Mentavio needs a curated beginner financial glossary based on authoritative educational sources.

The glossary should include real market terminology such as short selling, leverage, margin, portfolio, risk, volatility, order types, company fundamentals, and economic cycles.

The glossary must be available in the interface and used as Sophia's reviewed source of truth.

### Sophia trust and accuracy

Sophia must not become a generic search box or a robotic definition engine.

The AI mentor must:

- use reviewed knowledge;
- explain concepts in the user's current simulation context;
- avoid unsupported claims;
- admit uncertainty;
- explain downside and risk;
- avoid repetitive canned language;
- log enough context for quality evaluation;
- be tested against a reviewed question set.

The glossary and trust requirements are preserved together in:

- `docs/specs/SPEC-029-FINANCIAL-GLOSSARY-AND-AI-TRUST.md`

## External educational reference

IBKR Campus and the IBKR Traders' Glossary were identified as useful sources of structural ideas and terminology.

Use them as references for course structure, lesson progression, glossary coverage, beginner explanations, and linking education to practical simulated decisions.

Do not copy proprietary text verbatim and do not import broker-specific terminology that is irrelevant to Mentavio.
