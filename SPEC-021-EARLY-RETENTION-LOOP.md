# SPEC-021 - Early Retention Loop

## Goal

Design the first 7 days of the player experience after the first successful trade.

The goal is to turn a curious new player into a returning user by pacing rewards, learning, missions, market events, mistakes, and emotional moments.

## Core Idea

SPEC-020 proves:

- "I can do this."

SPEC-021 should teach:

- "Now I want to understand why it worked."
- "The market is deeper than the first guided win."
- "Coming back tomorrow gives me something new."

## 7-Day Structure Draft

### Day 1

- Guided first trade.
- First profit.
- First token reward.
- First achievement.
- Short explanation: the first win was guided, real decisions start next.

### Day 2

- Unlock a simple daily mission.
- Ask the player to read one news item.
- Second trade should be mostly safe but not fully guaranteed.

### Day 3

- Unlock Intel basics.
- Show buy pressure, trend, and company profile.
- Mission: buy a company after checking Intel.

### Day 4

- Introduce the first mild negative event.
- Teach that prices can fall.
- Mission: avoid panic and review the portfolio.

### Day 5

- Introduce a sector opportunity.
- One sector gets positive news, another gets pressure.
- Mission: compare two companies.

### Day 6

- Introduce the first emotional market move.
- A small panic or euphoria moment appears.
- Mission: make a decision after news plus chart.

### Day 7

- First mini-crisis.
- Player must use news, Intel, portfolio, and risk awareness.
- Reward: larger token bonus, weekly achievement, unlock next mode.

## Systems Needed

- Day counter per account.
- Daily reward calendar.
- Daily mission assignment.
- Feature unlock schedule.
- Guided event schedule.
- Retention metrics:
  - Day-1 return
  - Day-3 return
  - Day-7 completion
  - second trade rate
  - mission completion rate
  - session duration

## Product Rule

The first 7 days should not punish the player harshly.

Risk should appear gradually:

1. success;
2. curiosity;
3. analysis;
4. small mistake;
5. recovery;
6. emotional market move;
7. first real challenge.
