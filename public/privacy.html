Privacy Policy

Mentavio collects only the data needed to operate the educational simulation, authenticate users, preserve account progress, and improve product reliability.

Data We Currently Store

The current MVP may store email address, display name, password hash, email verification status, account token balance, login streak, simulated player profile, holdings, simulated trades, purchases, achievements, daily quest progress, referral and early-access records, and document-consent records.

Data We Do Not Need

Mentavio should not store payment card numbers, government IDs, biometric data, or real brokerage account credentials in the product database.

Security

Passwords are stored as hashes. API keys and secrets must stay on the server and must not be included in the browser client or APK.

Account Data Rights

Account export and deletion flows are planned. Until they are implemented, requests should be handled manually by the project owner.

Review Notice

This privacy policy is a working draft and must be reviewed before broad public launch or paid access.

Mentavio is an educational simulation. It is not a broker, exchange, gambling product, or financial adviser.
